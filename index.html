Our Story

From Waste
to Wonder

Fibranusa was born from a simple observation: billions of banana sheaths are discarded each year, while the fashion industry struggles to find sustainable alternatives to animal leather.

Our patented process transforms these agricultural byproducts into a luxurious, durable material — without harming animals or the planet.

The Material

How Our Leather is Made

Watch how discarded banana sheaths are transformed into premium bio-leather through our patented, chemical-free process — from farm to finished product.

01
Harvest
Banana sheaths are collected from partner farms in Central Java after the fruit harvest.
02
Process
Fibres are extracted, cleaned, and treated using our plant-based, zero-chemical method.
03
Craft
The resulting bio-leather sheet is cut and hand-crafted into each gift set component.
